# Narration

English · Kokoro af_heart · restrained, curious delivery.

## Frame 1

Why do these numbers keep appearing in nature? Meet Fibonacci.

## Frame 2

One, one, two, three, five, eight. Add the last two numbers to get the next.

## Frame 3

In many sunflowers, seed spirals can be counted in neighboring Fibonacci numbers. Pinecones often show similar patterns.

## Frame 4

Many plants place new growth near the golden angle: about one hundred thirty seven point five degrees. This spacing reduces repeated alignment.

## Frame 5

But a spiral is not automatically Fibonacci. Shells, storms, and galaxies can form spirals through different growth and physical processes.

## Frame 6

Fibonacci is a remarkable pattern in some living systems. Yet life and the cosmos follow many different rules. Look closely. Count. Then ask why.
