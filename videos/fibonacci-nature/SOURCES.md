# Scientific notes and asset provenance

Checked 2026-10-15. The reel is a short explanation, with schematic artwork;
it does not present measurements from a photographed specimen or galaxy.

| Claim | Primary source | Treatment in this reel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Many sunflowers exhibit Fibonacci parastichy counts, with exceptions. | Swinton, Ochu and the MSI Turing's Sunflower Consortium (2016), [Novel Fibonacci and non-Fibonacci structure in the sunflower](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.160091), [open manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4892450/). | Say “many,” give 34/55 as an illustrative pair, and explicitly state real counts vary. The drawn paths are illustrative, not a complete specimen count. |
| Plant arrangement includes spiral and other patterns; golden-angle divergence is common in spiral phyllotaxis. | Kuhlemeier (2017), [Phyllotaxis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28898658/), Current Biology 27, R882–R887, doi:10.1016/j.cub.2017.05.069. | Say “many plants,” never every plant or living being. Biological mechanisms are more complex than a single spacing diagram. |
| Logarithmic spirals occur in storms and galaxies with different physical origins. | NASA APOD, [Logarithmic Spirals](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2008-may-17-logarithmic-spirals/); NASA, [Galaxy types](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/galaxies/types/). | A schematic galaxy illustrates spiral geometry. Its three arms do not represent the Milky Way or a measured galaxy. A spiral shape alone establishes no Fibonacci or golden-ratio relation. |

The sequence uses F(1)=F(2)=1 and F(n)=F(n−1)+F(n−2). The omitted initial
zero is a standard sequence convention. φ=(1+√5)/2. The golden angle is
360°×(1−1/φ)≈137.507764°. Square areas sum to 21×13; their side lengths
are 13, 8, 5, 3, 2, 1, 1.

## Media

- All SVGs are authored locally from deterministic geometry by
  `scripts/build-fibonacci.mjs`. They are editable vector source, not Rive output.
- Speech uses the local [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx)
  0.6.0 adapter, Kokoro v1.0 FP16 and `af_heart`. Weights and voices come from the
  upstream `model-files-v1.1` GitHub release. No cloud voice cloning or Gemini
  generation was performed.
- Captions map source words to the model's phoneme durations. A one-to-one
  boundary-count assertion rejects mismatches. This is synthesis alignment,
  not independent automatic speech recognition. The raw phoneme timings remain
  beside every WAV for inspection.
- The quiet tonal bed is an original procedural pentatonic score generated by
  `scripts/fibonacci-audio.py`; no third-party music or stock footage is used.
- Source Serif 4 and JetBrains Mono are locally bundled via pinned Fontsource
  packages. Their OFL licenses accompany the fonts. GSAP 3.15.0 is locally bundled.
- Style and composition workflow: official [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes)
  plugin 0.8.161, `faceless-explainer`, `editorial-forest`, captions and assembly scripts.
